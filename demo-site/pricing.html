Common questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

What counts as an order?

Any new order created in Northwind, whether placed by a customer online or entered manually by your staff.

Do you charge transaction fees?

Never. You pay the flat monthly rate and nothing else. Payment processing fees from your payment provider are separate and unrelated to Northwind.
