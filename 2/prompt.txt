You build a product, you ship it, and you connect analytics.
Now you have a lot of data, but not sure what to do with it. 

Someone still has to read those numbers, guess what to change, edit the page, and come back later to see if it worked. That loop never keeps running on its own.

This was the case with my portfolio. The stats were there. But I wanted a continuous loop that turns them into a fix, ships it, and checks whether people stayed or not.

And That’s why I built Friction Fixer. Instead of guessing what to change and where, it looks at the actual data, makes a plan, executes it, and comes to checks back later. 

If the result improved, it keeps the change. If it did not, it undoes it and tries again. No guesswork required.
